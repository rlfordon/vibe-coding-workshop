What is vibe coding?

A way for non-technical users to create software or apps by describing what they want in natural language, while AI takes care of generating the code behind the scenes.

"There's a new kind of coding I call 'vibe coding', where you fully give in to the vibes, embrace exponentials, and forget the code even exists."

— Andrej Karpathy, Feb 2025

You talk to AI. AI writes code. You get a working tool.

MORITZ COLLEGE OF LAW 2

Why vibe code?

  • You're the domain expert. You know your students, your research, your classroom. That expertise is the hard part — the coding is now the easy part.
  • It builds real AI fluency. Being precise about what you want, iterating when results aren't right, evaluating output critically — these are the skills that matter for any professional AI use. And you get a useful tool at the end.
  • It changes what you're willing to try. When the cost of experimenting drops to near zero, you pursue ideas you'd never have proposed before. Build it in an afternoon, try it once, toss it or iterate.

Things to keep in mind

  • Student data & FERPA — don't put student names, grades, or identifiable info into AI tools or vibe-coded apps
  • Accessibility — AI-generated tools may not meet ADA standards; test and iterate, just like you'd proofread a handout
  • Vet before classroom use — AI-generated code can have bugs or wrong answers; run through it yourself first
  • Academic integrity — if students use these tools, be clear about what the tool is doing vs. what the student is doing
  • Not legal advice — if the tool touches legal topics, make sure students understand it's a teaching exercise

How to get started

  • Pick the smallest version of your idea
  • Open Gemini Canvas (free with your university Google account)
  • Describe what you want in plain language
  • Iterate — the first version is just the start
  • Share what you build with colleagues

You don't need to be a coder. You need to describe what you want clearly enough for AI to build it.

Free tools to keep building

You don't need to stop after today. All of these are free or have trials:

  • Gemini Canvas — free with your university Google account, great starting point
  • Google AI Studio — free with a personal Google account
  • Cursor — free for students for a year
  • Claude Code — Anthropic's coding tool
  • Replit — browser-based development with AI assistant
  • v0 by Vercel — describe a user interface, get working code
  • Lovable — full-stack app builder
  • Bolt.new — build and deploy in the browser
