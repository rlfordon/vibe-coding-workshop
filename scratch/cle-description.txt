Vibe Coding for Legal Professionals: Build a Custom Tool with AI in 60 Minutes

What if you could build a custom tool for your practice — a client intake form, a deadline tracker, a fee calculator — just by describing what you need in plain English? In this hands-on session, attendees will do exactly that, using AI to go from idea to a working web application in under an hour.

"Vibe coding" is the practice of describing what you want in natural language and letting AI generate the code. No programming knowledge required. Participants will craft prompts, iterate on designs, and deploy a real project — learning a skill that extends far beyond this session. The same techniques used to build a tool from scratch apply to automating routine workflows, creating custom dashboards, or prototyping solutions to practice-specific problems that off-the-shelf software doesn't solve.

For firms already using platforms like Clio or similar practice management systems, vibe coding offers a way to fill the gaps: building lightweight tools that connect the dots between existing systems, automate repetitive administrative tasks, or give clients a better experience — all without hiring a developer or waiting on a vendor roadmap.

Attendees will leave with a published project they built themselves, a reusable prompt toolkit, and a concrete understanding of what AI-assisted development can do for their practice — not as a theoretical concept, but as something they've done with their own hands.

Key Takeaways:
- How to use AI to build functional tools for your practice through natural language prompts
- Practical prompt engineering techniques that transfer to any AI tool or workflow
- A realistic sense of what AI can (and can't) build today, and where the efficiency gains are
- Hands-on experience that demystifies the technology behind legal tech products

To Participate:
Attorneys who want to build along during the session should bring a laptop and sign up for a free Google Gemini account at gemini.google.com prior to the session. A free account is all you need.
