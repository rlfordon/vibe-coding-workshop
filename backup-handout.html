Your Prompt Template

Copy this into Gemini (gemini.google.com) to start building. Replace the bracketed text with your idea.

I want to make a Gemini Canvas app. [DESCRIBE YOUR IDEA — what problem does it solve? who is it for?]

Before you start building, ask me a few questions about who this is for and what their experience should be like.

Canvas is Gemini's built-in code editor. Click the "Canvas" button under Tools when you're ready to build. If you don't see it, ask Gemini to write the code and it will give you something you can copy.

Steps to Follow

Identify a pain point

What process is repetitive, confusing, or error-prone? What information do people need in a more accessible format?

Open Gemini

Go to gemini.google.com with your university Google account. No signup needed.

Plan with AI

Don't just say "build me an app." Have a conversation first. Let Gemini ask you questions. Go back and forth until you both understand what you're building.

Build it

Copy a prompt from above (or write your own). Paste it into Gemini. When ready, click "Canvas" under Tools to start building.

Iterate

The first version is just the start. Ask for changes: better design, more content, edge case handling, mobile support.

Your Assignment

Pick your idea

  • Pick an idea card above — or bring your own
  • Copy the prompt

Sit in clusters

  • Groups of 4–6
  • Your cluster = your support group
  • Ask them for help before raising your hand

Goals

  • Plan with Gemini before you build
  • Get a working first version
  • Iterate — make it better
  • Test it on someone in your cluster

Session Plan

~2 min
Form clusters — groups of 4–6, pick your idea
~20 min
Build — first sprint — plan with Gemini, then start building
~3 min
Cluster check-in — show your cluster what you have so far
~20 min
Build — second sprint — keep iterating, level up
~5 min
Swap & test — try someone else's app, give feedback
~5 min
Whole-room share — volunteers demo (60 sec each)
